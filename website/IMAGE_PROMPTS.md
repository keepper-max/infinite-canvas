模式：内置 imagegen；每个项目独立生成一张新图，原截图仅作为参考，不是要逐像素保留的编辑目标。生成后复制到H盘项目，原截图不覆盖。最终文件为assets/cases/quality-concept-v1.png、factory-concept-v1.png、canvas-concept-v1.png。

Luminous pale ice-blue studio environment, restrained cyan edge light, transparent glass and matte ceramic materials, realistic soft shadows, generous breathing room, readable at thumbnail size. The focal point is the inspection workflow, not a screen filled with tiny text. Only optional short Chinese labels: “知识依据”, “包装审核”, “人工复核”. No other text, no numerical scores, no fake success metrics, no logos, no brand endorsement, no watermark, no people. Do not simply put the screenshot inside a laptop. Crisp clean architectural art direction, sophisticated not cluttered sci-fi.

Supplied screenshot is a reference for a real connected video node and its media controls, NOT an edit target. Create a new beautiful premium visualization of a node-based AI creative workspace: three large airy floating media panels linked by delicate clear cyan curves, showing a written creative brief as restrained typographic lines, an original cinematic image of misty mountains and a small pavilion, and a video-preview panel continuing that same mountain scene with a minimal playback bar.
